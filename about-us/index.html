About Natasha Jones

Solicitor, founder of The Effective Law Group and your point of contact from the first call.

Experience you can lean on

The Effective Law Group is headed up by Natasha Jones, who was featured for a number of years in The Legal 500 Who’s Who in the Law. She has nearly 30 years of experience, advising on all areas of employment law, including dismissals, strategic HR issues, protection of confidential information and post-termination restrictions.

Natasha’s strengths lie in her ability to create bespoke solutions and always manage a project to completion. Her reputation is one of tenacity. She has regularly written for legal publications and has featured in BBC radio debates.
