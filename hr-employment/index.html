Bespoke HR and employment law advice

While you focus on running your business, your dedicated legal professional works as part of your team, for a fixed cost.

Support that fits your business

We offer a tailored package with as much or as little support as you want. It can include:

  • Unlimited email
  • Face-to-face meetings
  • Telephone support
  • Training and seminars
  • Review and drafting of contracts
  • A unique handbook, with policies drafted around your company’s needs

The full spectrum of HR

We cover recruitment, contracts of employment and handbooks, performance management, maternity leave, disciplinary and grievance issues, redundancy and reorganisation, and day-to-day HR matters. With our flexible approach, nothing is too big or too small.

How we work with you

We can work with you on site, or remotely, to manage and resolve the issues in front of you, making the most of your people while protecting your business. We work with organisations across all sectors.
